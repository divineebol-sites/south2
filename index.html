    Before you reserve

    Common questions

    Who is the developer?

    SM Prime Holdings, Inc., per SMDC's June 2026 briefing. Property management is by Greenmist Property Management Corporation.

    When is the turnover?

    The June 2026 briefing projects Q4 2026: Tower 3 in August 2026, and Towers 1 and 2 in October 2026. Ask me for the latest construction update.

    What comes with the unit?

    Ceramic floor tiles, painted walls, laminated wood doors, a fire-rated main door, aluminum windows, ceramic wall tiles in the toilet and bath, and a granite kitchen countertop with sink and base and overhead cabinets.

    How many elevators are there?

    Two passenger elevators and one service elevator per tower, plus two fire exit stairs per tower.

    Who provides the utilities?

    Power from Meralco and water from Maynilad.

    Is there parking?

    Yes, 345 slots in total (264 open and 81 basement) for 1,938 units, so parking is limited. Ask me early if you need a slot.

    Where is the showroom?

    2nd Floor, SM Southmall, Las Piñas City, right beside the project.
